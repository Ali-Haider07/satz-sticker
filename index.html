Satz-Sticker

Type a German line and add it to the sheet. Print the sheet, cut out the codes and stick them next to your notes. Scanning a code on your phone shows the line, the translation and a German voice.

This must be the link where this page lives, because your phone opens it when you scan. If you move the page (for example to GitHub Pages), paste the new link here and print the stickers again.
